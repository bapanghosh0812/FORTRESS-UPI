Things to try

  • Scan & pay: the scanner locks onto the shop's QR and opens the payment.
  • Pay a friend: review, then confirm on the simulated NPCI PIN screen.
  • Go offline: set Network to “No internet”, then use Tap & Pay from the vault.
  • Come back online: offline payments settle and your change returns to the bank.
  • Fraud shield: open the “KYC refund” request on Home.
  • Settings: Profile has accounts, UPI PIN, limits, security, privacy and help.
Example data only. No real money moves. The PIN step is simulated: in the real app NPCI's secure library collects your UPI PIN and Fortress never sees it. The Android app in this project runs the same flows against a sandbox PSP.
